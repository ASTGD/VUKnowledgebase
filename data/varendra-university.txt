----------------------------------------------------------------------

2. INSTITUTE HISTORY & FOUNDING
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Motivation: Rajshahi is known as the "city of education"; more than one
  lakh (100,000) students apply to Rajshahi University annually but only
  about 5,000 are admitted, and public universities average more than 20
  candidates per seat, causing "session jam". The sponsors proposed a
  private university to provide quality higher education to this region.
- Founder / initiative: Mr. Hafizur Rahaman Khan, Honorable Chairman of
  VUT (Varendra University Trust), convened a meeting of distinguished
  educationists and social workers at the proposed campus in Rajshahi
  city. He proposed establishing a private university in a calm and
  tranquil location "away from the madding crowd"; the proposal was
  accepted unanimously.
- Historical inspiration: the university claims the legacy of the historic
  learning centers of the Varendra region, e.g. Somapura Mahavihara of
  Paharpur (770–810 AD) and the Darasbari Madrasha at Nawabganj
  (15th century AD).
- Logo: designed by Architect Tariq Mahbub Khan, PhD. The square logo
  abstractly represents the physical layout of Varendra's historic centers
  of learning; courtyard = sharing/exchange of knowledge, rooms =
  individual/group study. Colors: brown (typical soil color of the
  Varendra area), gray and black; name in Bangla and English.
- Campus construction: 44 bighas acquired for the permanent campus on
  Rajshahi Bypass Road, Chandrima, Paba, Rajshahi; construction began
  March 14, 2018; the first phase was completed in 2022; classes are
  currently conducted on the permanent campus. (Older/legacy text also
  mentions a 54-bigha plan at Kharkhori Bypass More and earlier rented
  campuses at Kazla/Motihar and Talaimary.)

  engineering/pharmacy/business programs, OR three semesters per year

6.3 Undergraduate admission requirements (general)
- Honors programs: total GPA 5.00 with minimum 2.5 in either SSC or
  HSC/equivalent. Students with minimum GPA 2.00 in SSC/HSC but total
  GPA 6.00+ may also apply — EXCEPT CSE, EEE, Pharmacy and Law and
  Human Rights departments.
- O/A Level: minimum 5 O-Level + 2 A-Level subjects; must have B grade
  (GPA 4.00) in 4 of 7 subjects and C grade (GPA 3.50) in the other 3.
- Freedom Fighter quota: children of freedom fighters with total GPA
  5.00 in both SSC and HSC.
- Science groups: School of Science and Technology + School of
  Engineering candidates必须 pass HSC/Diploma from science group.
- Diploma holders from government/UGC/council-approved institutions are
  treated as HSC-equivalent but must take the usual admission test.
- HSC result currency: Pharmacy candidates must complete admission within
  3 years of HSC result publication. (Others: within 2 years is the
  published norm for many programs — verify per program.)

6.4 Department-specific requirements
- CSE & EEE: GPA 3.00 in both SSC and HSC; science group; minimum GPA 3.00
  in Mathematics (scale of 5.00) in HSC. VU VIVA Test scores accepted
  (EEE); US high school diploma accepted.
- Law and Human Rights: GPA 3.00 in both SSC and HSC.
- Pharmacy (B.Pharm): total GPA 8.00 (scale of 10.00) in SSC+HSC from
  science group, minimum 3.50 (scale of 5.00) in EACH exam; minimum GPA
  3.50 in Chemistry & Biology and 3.00 in Physics in HSC; minimum 3.00 in
  Mathematics (students without it take an extra 3-credit Maths course).
  A-Level: B in Chemistry & Biology, C in Physics & Math. Diploma-in-
  Pharmacy holders apply via the VU Department of Pharmacy.

6.5 Graduate (Masters) admission point system
- Minimum 5.00 points with no third division/class; or 6.00 points with a
  third division/class. Minimum CGPA 2.00 at each level.
- Points table: SSC/HSC/Degree pass: 1st div 3, 2nd div 2, 3rd div 1;
  Honors/4-year degree: 4/3/2.
- MPH extra: MBBS with 2 years practical exposure + BMDC registration, OR
  4-yr degree with GPA 2.5+ in relevant subjects (pharmacy, botany,
  zoology, biochemistry, psychology, veterinary, fisheries, microbiology,
  genetics, social sciences, anthropology, economics), OR 3-yr honors
  degree with at least 2nd class, OR masters in a health subject.
  Non-medical students take a written MCQ + viva + English placement test.

8. SCHOLARSHIPS AND WAIVERS
----------------------------------------------------------------------
- Scale: approx. 16,000 students have received tuition scholarships to
  date; approx. TK 4 crore per year in financial assistance to poor and
  meritorious students (Pro-VC statement).
- Waiver circular (Summer-2026 intake onwards, portal image
  http://160.187.25.3:8083/img/Waivers.jpg — Bengali; [VERIFY] exact
  thresholds): merit categories (ক/খ/গ/ঘ/ঙ/চ/ছ) with waivers of
  approximately 70%, 20%, 10% etc., keyed to SSC+HSC GPA thresholds
  (several categories use total GPA 7.40; others 2.50, 9.00, 7.00);
  100% waiver for students with disabilities; approx. 95% for children of
  freedom fighters; 75% tuition waiver / full free studentship holders get
  no additional benefit; a student may hold only ONE waiver at a time.
- Waiver application process: apply through the STUDENT PANEL
  ("Waiver Application" → fill → SUBMIT — without pressing Submit the
  application is not processed); contact the department head if denied.
- Free Studentship notices are published per cycle per department (e.g.,
  "Free Studentship (Summer-2026)" notice 3295, June 16, 2026).
- International students: first two admitted students in each department
  get 100% tuition + development fee waiver; all other international
  students get a 75% tuition fee waiver, which is retained in later
  semesters by maintaining minimum GPA 3.50.

16. GRADING SYSTEM (UGC uniform scale)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
A+ = 4.00 (80%+); A = 3.75 (75–<80); A− = 3.50 (70–<75); B+ = 3.25
(65–<70); B = 3.00 (60–<65); B− = 2.75 (55–<60); C+ = 2.50 (50–<55);
C = 2.25 (45–<50); D = 2.00 (40–<45); F = 0.00 (<40). A+ through D are
pass grades. Engineering adds X (thesis continuation), I (incomplete),
W (withdrawn). CGPA = Σ(credits × grade points) / total credits.
